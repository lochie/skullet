The first release: skeletons measured from your own content, so nothing moves when it loads.

#### New

- `SkeletonReveal` and `SkeletonSuspense` for React, `SkeletonReveal` for Vue and Svelte, and `Reveal` for plain JavaScript.
- Skeletons drawn from `sample` data, remembered shapes or server-rendered HTML — painted before the first frame.
- `smart`, `together`, `sequence` and `sweep` reveals.
- Theming: light and dark colours, radius, shimmer, group shimmer or pulse, and speed.
